Does this machine join a desk?

A desk is the group of machines that share keyboard, mouse, monitors and work. This one is “MacBook Pro”.

Found on your network
PC-DesktopWindows · desk “My desk” · 1 machine · over Wi-Fi

On joining, this machine trusts every machine of the desk and they trust it, with administrator access for remote compute. You can remove it later from any machine; the other one forgets its key only if connected, otherwise once it also leaves the desk.

Then, on the other machine, choose “Join a desk” and enter the code that will appear here.
