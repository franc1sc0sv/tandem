# T-347 — A channel from the Mac at all times

## Context

The 34" only answers DDC on HDMI while HDMI is the active input (`monitor-firmware`, `docs/targets/lc34g55t/journal.md`: "requests on the inactive port are most likely never processed"). So while it shows the PC, the Mac has no channel and depends on the PC. The user authorized on 2026-11-19 the work to patch the firmware so that the DDC/CI service also answers on the inactive HDMI input. That work lives in the `monitor-firmware` repo; a flash still needs the user's go-ahead at the moment of flashing.

## Scope (once the firmware ships it)

1. Pin the new `monitor-ddc` commit and recognize the firmware protocol version that answers on the inactive input.
2. The Mac keeps its channel while the 34" shows the PC; election and T-346 work unchanged.
3. Hardware check of the whole loop from the Mac with the 34" on the PC: live state, events, switch to HDMI.

## Acceptance criteria

- To be defined with the firmware's protocol change.
