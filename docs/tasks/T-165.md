# T-165 — Missing SAFETY comments on FFI unsafe blocks

## Context

Failure found by the acceptance agent, part C (2026-11-18, `origin/develop` 04f2967). See `docs/reports/acceptance-2026-11-18-c.md`.

## Failure

Rust convention (CLAUDE.md): `unsafe` only for FFI and with `// SAFETY:`. Of 162 uses of `unsafe` in the repo, these 7 do not have the comment:

- `crates/tandem-peripherals/src/macos.rs:25` and `:43` (`unsafe extern "C"` of IOKit and CoreFoundation).
- `crates/tandem-input/src/macos/capture/sys.rs:38` and `:55` (ApplicationServices and Carbon) and `:290` (`unsafe extern "C-unwind" fn tap_callback`).
- `crates/tandem-files/src/drag_macos.rs:111` and `:113` (`unsafe impl NSObjectProtocol` / `NSDraggingSource`).

## Expected fix

Add a `// SAFETY:` (or `# Safety` on the function) that says why it is valid: signature verified against the SDK, pointers that do not outlive the call, the thread the callback runs on, etc. Without changing behavior. A CI script (`scripts/`) that fails on an `unsafe` without a nearby `SAFETY` keeps it from coming back.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The 7 sites carry their comment.
- [x] `cargo fmt`, `clippy -D warnings` and `cargo test --workspace` green (macOS included in CI).

## Notes

- Besides the 7 sites listed, `scripts/ci/safety.sh` found 2 more on Windows (`keyboard_proc` in `tandem-input/src/windows/capture/sys.rs` and `collect` in `.../windows/emulate/sys.rs`); they also carry a comment.
