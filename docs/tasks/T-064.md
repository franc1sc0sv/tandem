# T-064 — In-flight steps fail when their link drops (PEER_LOST)

## Scope

A step sent to the peer over a link that drops waits for its full timeout (10 s) even though the manager detects the drop in ~1.25 s; DESIGN §6 asks not to wait for long timeouts (QA round 2 observation).
When the drop of the link a step travels over is detected, fail it immediately with `PEER_LOST` as an uncertain failure, so the transaction retries or rolls back over the next link.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Test in tandem-sim: the step's link drops midway → the step fails in < 2 s with PEER_LOST and the transaction ends consistent.
- [x] Paused tokio clock; no real sleeps.

## Notes

- `tandemd::peer::over_link` runs a request to the other agent over a link and fails it with `PEER_LOST` (uncertain failure) as soon as the link manager considers that link down (or it closes). tandem-sim's `ManagedPeer` uses it for every step; the real agent will have to use it when it connects the link manager to its requests.
- Failing early opens a race: the inverse arrives over the next link while the original step is still running on the other agent. That is why `Orchestrator::execute_for_peer` now serves the other agent's steps one at a time even if they arrive over different streams (the wait is bounded by the maximum time of the step in progress).
- Tests: `caida_del_enlace_con_un_paso_a_mitad_falla_enseguida` (tandem-sim, fails in ~1.25 s), `peer::tests::request_fails_when_its_link_goes_down` and related, and `orchestrator::tests::peer_steps_from_different_streams_run_one_at_a_time`. `caida_del_enlace_preferido_con_la_respuesta_en_el_aire` now expects `PEER_LOST` instead of "no response".
