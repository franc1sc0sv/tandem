# T-234 — Time limits in `release.yml`

## Context

Failure found by the acceptance agent, part C, round 3 (2026-11-19, `origin/develop` bbdc245). See `docs/reports/acceptance-2026-11-18-c3.md`. T-191 asked that no CI job wait for GitHub's 6 h limit.

## Failure

`ci.yml` has `timeout-minutes` on all 7 jobs (PASS). In `.github/workflows/release.yml` the jobs `artifacts` and `release` have none (only `prepare`, `macos` and `windows` do).

## Expected fix

Add `timeout-minutes` (for example 15) to both jobs.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Every job in `.github/workflows/*.yml` has `timeout-minutes` (check with a script in `scripts/ci/`, run in `ci.yml`).
- [x] Quality gate and CI green.

## Notes

- `artifacts` and `release` get 15 minutes (recent runs take under a minute). `scripts/ci/timeouts.sh` fails on any job without `timeout-minutes`; it runs in the Linux CI step next to `tasks.sh`.
