# T-062 — Adversarial QA round

## Scope

Recurring task: read what has been integrated since the last round, look for uncovered cases (edges, errors, concurrency) and add tests. If a new test fails, open the fix in the same PR.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] At least 10 significant new tests per round or a note explaining why they were not needed.
- [x] The bugs found are listed in the PR.

## Notes

Recurring task: for another round, set `status: todo` again and uncheck the criteria.

- **Round 1** (#16): T-002, T-003, T-004, T-005, T-007, T-010, T-020, T-021, T-061. 26 tests, 4 bugs.
- **Round 2**: T-006, T-008, T-011, T-012, T-014, T-022, T-023, T-024, T-026, T-029, T-060, end to end with `tandem-sim`. 15 new tests (one property test over mode sequences with injected faults), 4 bugs fixed:
  1. The Mac and the PC could orchestrate at the same time over the same desk: now a machine that performs steps for the other is lent out until it receives its final state (or until the loan expires) and a machine that is orchestrating rejects foreign steps.
  2. A power off with no response was undone and the Mac took its monitors away: now it is taken as done.
  3. and 4. A DDC input change or a desktop step that timed out kept running on its blocking thread and could finish after its inverse: now they wait their turn.
- Observations for the backlog (no change):
  - After a PC power cut, the Mac agent stays with `pc_on = true`: no plan sends Wake-on-LAN again and the 34" on DisplayPort stays black until the PC is turned on by hand. This falls to T-030 (or whoever reconciles `pc_on` with the links).
  - An in-flight step over a link that drops waits for its maximum time (10 s) even though the manager detects the drop in 1.25 s. The manager also has no hysteresis when returning to the preferred link: an intermittent USB4 can end in `Inconsistent` even though Ethernet is healthy.
