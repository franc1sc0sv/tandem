# T-143 — Reduce the covered areas of `shots-spec.ts` to those with a strong reason

## Context

Finding of the acceptance agent (part A): review of each covered region of `apps/tandem-ui/scripts/shots-spec.ts` against `mockups/README.md`. The README's rule is "only a box that cannot match (real data, a function that does not exist) is covered, and never a title, text or button that the app does have". These have a strong reason (D1, D2, D17, G4, nonexistent function, mockup defect): "Add machine", "Simulate…", "Stop waiting", "Send over Wi-Fi anyway", "Lock here", "Remove from desk", "Latency · 10 min" (60 s), MCP sessions, the desk name field and the rectangles of the `.cur` class of `F-Asis5`. These do not:

1. **Palette (`F-Palette`):** whole rows that the app does have are covered: "Monitors", "Network" and the "Go to" label (they receive the shift that the tunnels cause) and, with "real data", the scenes ("Apply 'PC only'"), "Go to tunnel 'ia-local'" and "'tandem'" and the color dot of "Work": eight boxes, ≈ 16 % of the screenshot. The demo already seeds the mockup's texts in `?states` and `?notices`; here it could do the same.
2. **Tray (`V3-Tray`):** "real data" covers the link and its latency, the state and load of each machine and the tunnels (17.8 % of the image, with their status dots and "active/inactive"). In the demo they are not real data: they can be seeded like the mockup's (`USB-C · 0.4 ms`, `macOS`, `2 tunnels`, "Mac → PC"; the "Mac"/"PC" labels come from `shortLabels`).
3. **This machine (`F-MachineMac`):** `.mod > :last-child` also covers the "Open settings" button of the "Permissions" row, which the app does draw; the README only justifies the module state (G4).
4. **Setup (`F-Asis5`):** the 640 × 50 px box covers the whole paragraph, including the sentences the app says the same ("This is the arrangement Tandem read. Drag if it does not match your desk and test…"); only the machine names differ.

## Scope

1. Seed the mockup's data in the demo (`?palette`, `?tray`, or extending `tunnels=notas`) and remove masks 1 and 2 from `shots-spec.ts`, except those for D1 ("Add machine…", "Lock here").
2. Narrow the module row's mask so that it leaves out "Open settings", and that of the `F-Asis5` paragraph to the machine names.
3. Update `mockups/README.md` with what remains covered and its reason, and with the measured similarity.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] No mask covers a title, text or button that the app has, except those on the list of strong reasons.
- [x] `palette`, `tray`, `machine-a` and `setup-arrangement*` stay at ≥ 0.99 / ≥ 0.97 with less covered area (compare against `docs/reports/acceptance/2026-11-18-a/masks.txt`).
- [x] Quality gate and CI green.
